# Introducing Greenulos™: The Future of Nanocellulose

*Published on August 15, 2024 by Dr. Soumitra Hazra*

## Revolutionizing Industries with Sustainable Nanocellulose

At ChemActiva, we're proud to introduce **Greenulos™**, our flagship carboxylated crystalline nano cellulose product that represents the cutting edge of sustainable materials science. This revolutionary material combines exceptional performance with minimal environmental impact, setting new standards for what sustainable materials can achieve.

## What Makes Greenulos™ Special?

Greenulos™ is sodium salt of carboxylated crystalline nano cellulose, available in both powder and liquid forms. Its unique properties make it ideal for a wide range of industrial applications:

### Exceptional Absorption Capacity
- **Oil Absorption**: Up to 50 times its weight in oil
- **Water Absorption**: Superior water retention capabilities
- **Selective Absorption**: Prefers oil over water, making it perfect for spill cleanup

### Superior Material Properties
- **High Surface Area**: Enhanced reactivity and performance
- **Biodegradable**: Completely breaks down in natural environments
- **Non-Toxic**: Safe for human contact and ecosystems
- **Renewable**: Derived from sustainable plant sources

## Applications Across Industries
